# Every moment Aim speaks up

Aim should feel simple even where it isn't. Every card, question, tip and offer is listed here, with
what it's for and how loud it is. The rule: **interrupt only to protect the aim; everything else
waits to be asked for, or speaks in one quiet line.** Nothing was removed to get here. Features moved
behind a tap, folded into one card, or stopped firing when the answer was obvious.

Loudness, from loudest to quietest:

- **Stop:** a card over the player or the results. Something must be chosen.
- **Card:** a dismissible notice. It can be ignored.
- **Line:** one line of text with a link and an ×.
- **On request:** appears only when you open it.

## What "feel simple" means here

- **One question per stop.** A stop asks one thing, with the likely answer first. Everything else
  folds under *More options*.
- **Don't ask what you can infer.** Same channel, same words, a title that matches: it's part of the
  aim. Asking would teach people to click through without reading.
- **Every no can become a yes.** Off-aim cards always offer *Yes, it's part of it*; Done always offers
  *Pick it back up*.
- **Offers are lines, not cards.** An invitation that can wait gets one line and an ×.
- **Setup is a glance.** Good defaults, already on, and the full designer for anyone who wants it.
